Who We Are

HopeForward NGO

HopeForward is a non-governmental organization dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities across India. Founded with the belief that every person deserves dignity, health, and opportunity, we have been working on the ground to create meaningful change.

Our multidisciplinary approach combines education, healthcare, and livelihood programs to address the root causes of poverty and inequality.

Our Mission

To create a lasting impact in the lives of underprivileged children and communities by providing education, women empowerment, and livelihood opportunities.

Our Vision

A world where every child has access to quality education, healthcare, and the opportunity to achieve their full potential in a just and equitable society.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, compassion, and accountability guide every action we take and every rupee we spend in service of our mission.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

2010 – Founded

HopeForward was established with a small team of 5 volunteers in Mumbai, starting with a literacy program for 100 children.

2013 – Healthcare Expansion

Launched our first mobile health unit, bringing medical care to 10 remote villages in Maharashtra.

2016 – Livelihood Program

Started vocational training centres for women, training over 2,000 women in marketable skills annually.

2020 – COVID Relief

Distributed relief kits to over 10,000 families during the pandemic and launched online education resources.

2022 – 50K Milestone

Reached the milestone of impacting 50,000 lives across 20 districts in 5 states.

2024 – Digital Platform

Launched our digital CMS platform to improve transparency, engagement, and donor trust.
